# Piper voice licenses - commercial use audit

Generated by fetching every `MODEL_CARD` from the `rhasspy/piper-voices` catalog (176 voices) and reading the License field, with a handful verified against the actual source license (not just the MODEL_CARD text) where it mattered most - see "Verified" rows below.

**Not legal advice.** This is a best-effort audit of publicly stated licenses; verify anything you plan to rely on commercially yourself, especially the "needs review" section.

## Critical: the default English voice is NOT commercial-safe

`en_US-lessac-*` (low/medium/high) - **the script's default `--language en` voice** (`DEFAULT_VOICE_BY_LANGUAGE["en"]` in `tts_batch/backends/piper.py`) - is built on the Blizzard 2013 / Lessac corpus, whose license (verified directly against cstr.ed.ac.uk) explicitly excludes "any commercial purpose, including the development, marketing, commercialisation, sale or licencing of voice synthesis or speech recognition products." If you've been generating with the default voice and intend commercial use, switch voices.
